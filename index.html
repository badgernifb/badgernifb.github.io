Part 1

Man's Sinful Condition

"As it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one:" — Romans 3:10

"For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God;" — Romans 3:23

According to those verses, everyone has sinned, which means that everyone has broken God's divine laws. Before one can receive salvation, they must admit that they are a sinner.

Part 2

The Consequence of Sin

"For the wages of sin is death..." — Romans 6:23a

"But the fearful, and unbelieving, and the abominable, and murderers, and whoremongers, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all liars, shall have their part in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone: which is the Second Death." — Revelation 21:8

"The same shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God... and he shall be tormented with fire and brimstone... And the smoke of their torment ascendeth up for ever and ever: and they have no rest day nor night..." — Revelation 14:10-11

Because we are all sinners, we have earned a wage, that is, death. However, "death" is not just "lights out" as some would like to believe. Rather, death is when one's soul descends into Hell to be tormented. This torment lasts forever and ever, and the Bible says that all liars shall have their part in this torment.

Part 3

Christ's Deity, Sacrifice & Resurrection

"For there are three that bear record in heaven, the Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost: and these three are one." — 1 John 5:7

"But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us." — Romans 5:8

"For I delivered unto you first of all that which I also received, how that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; And that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures:" — 1 Corinthians 15:3-4

Even though we are sinners worthy of Hellfire, God still loves us. However, in order to understand this, you must understand what is commonly referred to as the "Trinity." Jesus Christ himself is God made manifest in the flesh, but is also God's son at the same time. How can this be? There is one Godhead that is made up of three distinct beings; the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. They are three different persons, but are one God.

So, he sent his one and only son, the Lord Jesus Christ, to die on the cross for our sins. When the Son, Jesus Christ, died on the cross, he took all of the sins of the whole world upon himself as if he had done it. Every lie, murder, and cheat. On the third day, however, he rose again out of the grave and defeated death.

Part 4

Received By Faith Alone

"For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast." — Ephesians 2:8-9

"But to him that worketh not, but believeth on him that justifieth the ungodly, his faith is counted for righteousness." — Romans 4:5

Salvation is a free gift. Now that you understand how the gift was purchased, you can understand how to receive it. According to the Bible, all you need to do is believe on Jesus to be saved; nothing more, nothing left. You do not need to go to church every Sunday, be baptized, turn from/repent of your sins, or become a good person. The free gift of eternal life is just that---free. It cannot be earned by good works whatsoever. If you have 99% of your trust in what Jesus did on the cross to save you, but 1% of trust in yourself, then you are not saved. You must lean entirely on Jesus Christ to save you, not yourself.

Part 5

Calling On The Lord

"That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation." — Romans 10:9-10

"For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved." — Romans 10:13

The Bible gives us clear instructions on how to put our trust in Jesus Christ. First, you must believe in the Gospel. That is the death, burial, and resurrection of Jesus Christ. Secondly, you must confess Jesus, which simply means to call upon him and ask him to save you. If you were in the water drowning, and you saw someone walking by the water, what would you do? You would call out to him, of course! If you do that, then Jesus Christ will surely save you.

Part 6

Permanently Preserved

"And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand." — John 10:28

"In hope of eternal life, which God, that cannot lie, promised before the world began;" — Titus 1:2

Once you have believed in and called upon Jesus Christ to be your personal saviour, there is absolutely nothing that can separate you from him. Once you are saved, you are always saved. The Bible says that if you believe on Jesus, you are completely sealed and can never die or lose the free gift of eternal life that God has given you. Since God has already promised the saved eternal life before the world began, he could not ever break that promise or go back on it, otherwise, he would be a liar (which cannot be the case according to Titus 1:2).
